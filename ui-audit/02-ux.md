# Phase 2: UX and consistency audit (`apps/app`)

Read-only audit, 2026-11-04, against commit `9ff266c`. Paths are relative to `apps/app/src/` unless they start with `apps/` or a repo-root file. It builds on [01-inventory.md](01-inventory.md): the design direction in §0 there (yellow as a signal, Inter, 6px radius, true-black dark mode, analytics scoped to the active business) is taken as decided, and component names (`DataTable`, `RecordSheet`, `PageHeader`, `EmptyState`, `StatStrip`, `ConfirmDeleteDialog`, …) refer to the shared components proposed there.

Finding IDs use the `UX-` prefix. The four defects from the Phase 1 appendix keep their `INV-A*` IDs and are written up in full here (§3, §4). Severity is **High** (data loss, a broken or blocked core task, or an accessibility barrier with no workaround), **Medium** (real friction or a visible inconsistency) or **Low** (polish).

## Summary

- **Core journeys work on the happy path, but break or dead-end at the edges.** Saves have no pending state and don't navigate anywhere, so it's easy to create duplicates. Errors only appear as toasts, and some (delete, status change, invoice edit) aren't caught at all. Two bugs lose data: editing an invoice erases its notes (INV-A1), and a failed save can clear the form (INV-A2).
- **The product doesn't do the last step of two of its four core journeys.** Invoices can't be sent from the app: you download a PDF, email it yourself, then set the status by hand. Contracts are sent through Firma but never show as signed, although marketing promises exactly that ("contracts (did they sign?!)"). These are product gaps rather than UI fixes, but the UI should stop implying otherwise.
- **The vocabulary disagrees with marketing and with itself.** Marketing sells *bookings* and *clients*; the app says Events and Customers, and the Overview and help text say "gigs" and "performance contracts". **Recommendation: Bookings and Clients in the UI**, without renaming collections or (for now) routes (§1.1).
- **Lists are the weakest pattern on mobile and for keyboard users.** Rows open details only on mouse click, the detail card appears below the pagination (off-screen on a phone), and the invoice status menu can't be reached by keyboard.
- **Several screens are broken below ~640px:** the analytics header overflows, the calendar shows four months side by side, and invoice payment rows lose their labels.
- **shadcn/Radix fixes some of this for free** (focus management, `Select` semantics, Sheet/Drawer, AlertDialog focus). The rest (row keyboard access, field errors, labels for custom controls, contrast) needs the shared components to be built right once (§4.3).

## 6. Friction in the core journeys

Each journey was traced through the code, starting from a signed-in user with one business.

### 6.1 Add a client

Sidebar → Customers → Create Customer → fill 12 fields in three sections → Save → toast "Customer saved successfully"; the form clears and you stay on the create page.

| ID | Sev | Friction | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| UX-61 | Medium | No way back to what you just created and no next step. The most common next step, a booking for this client, means going to the sidebar and finding them again in a picker. | After create: open the client in the list's `RecordSheet`, with a primary "New booking for Jane Doe" action. |
| UX-62 | Medium | Email is required, and so are company-or-contact names in the wrong order (UX-28, UX-32). The form shows all 12 fields, including a full UK-style address, for what is often a name and a phone number. | Name + email-or-phone first; address and notes in a collapsed "More details" section. |
| UX-63 | Low | Creating a client from inside the booking form throws the booking away (UX-31). | Inline "New client…" dialog from the picker. |

### 6.2 Create a booking (event)

Sidebar → Events → Create Event → title, start date + time, end date + time, customer, venue, notes → Save → toast "Event created"; the form keeps its values and you stay on the page.

| ID | Sev | Friction | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| UX-64 | High | Duplicate risk: the form stays filled and enabled after saving (UX-18, UX-19). | Navigate to the new booking; disable while saving. |
| UX-65 | High | Past dates are blocked and an empty date silently becomes "now" (UX-29); the location must be re-picked on every booking (UX-30). | As above. |
